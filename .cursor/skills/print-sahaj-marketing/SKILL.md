# PrintSahaj marketing

## Differentiator

Technology + marketing + automation as one system, not ads on the side.

Official pillar name: **Digital Growth & Marketing**. Do not call the pillar "Digital Growth", "Digital Marketing", "Marketing", or "Growth".

Traffic → landing page → lead → CRM → qualification → follow-up → conversion → reporting.

## Say

Lead generation, lead management, qualification, conversion websites, landing pages, branding, SEO, search visibility, content strategy, digital marketing, marketing automation, analytics, CRM integration, conversion optimization.

## Do not say

"We run ads." "Social media packages." Empty agency superlatives. Invented ROI, traffic, or client counts.

PrintSahaj is not a "verification system for India's printing and packaging industry." That wording is PrintVerify-only.

## CTA

Not "Need a website?"

- Have a business problem worth solving?
- Tell us what you're trying to improve. We'll help you figure out whether the answer is software, automation, marketing, or something else.
- Start a Project

## Claims

Never invent a client, project, product, feature, result, metric, testimonial, technology, business outcome, launch status, capability, certification, award, or case-study claim. If uncertain: CONTENT GAP — NEEDS VERIFICATION. Do not infer.

Status enum: Live | Building | Concept | Internal | Not publicly disclosed. Live only with verified evidence that the work is live and publicly presentable. Do not assume permission to display a client name because a project exists.
